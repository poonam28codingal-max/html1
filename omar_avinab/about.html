About Our School

Green Valley Public School is committed to providing quality education in a safe and nurturing environment. Our school focuses on academic excellence, character development, creativity, and overall personality growth. With experienced teachers and modern classrooms, we prepare students for a bright future.










School Library

Our library offers thousands of books, magazines, and digital resources to encourage reading and research. Students enjoy a peaceful learning environment where they can improve their knowledge and develop a love for books.










Sports & Activities

We believe in the overall development of every student. Our school provides facilities for football, basketball, cricket, athletics, dance, music, art, and many extracurricular activities that help students discover their talents and build confidence.










Why Choose Our School?

Qualified Teachers
